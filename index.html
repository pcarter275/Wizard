How a trick is won

Follow the led suit if you can. A Wizard or Jester may be played any time.

The first Wizard played takes the trick. Otherwise the highest trump wins, and with no trump in it, the highest card of the led suit. Jesters never win unless the trick is all Jesters — then the first one does.

Lead a Wizard and the others may play anything. Lead a Jester and the suit is set by the next real card.

Make your bid exactly for 20 plus 10 a trick. Miss by any amount and it's 10 off for each.
